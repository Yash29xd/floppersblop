Privacy Policy

Effective date31 October 2026
Who is responsibleYash Arya, an individual, operating FloppersBlop from New Delhi, India. It is not registered with any authority. It is a personal project hosted on GitHub Pages.
Contact for privacy questions29aryash@gmail.com
01What this policy covers

This policy explains what information the FloppersBlop website handles and why. It doesn’t cover other sites you reach through links, such as Modrinth, YouTube, Discord or Instagram. Those have their own policies.

02What is collected
InformationPosition
Messages you send by emailYour email address, your name if you give it, and what you write. Collected only if you write to me.
Contact formThe site has no contact form.
Technical dataGitHub, which hosts the site on GitHub Pages, may log technical data such as your IP address and browser details to deliver and protect the service. I don’t receive or use that data.
FontsThe pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser requests them from Google, so Google receives your IP address and browser details.
Cookies and analyticsNone. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising tools.
Stored in your browserThe site briefly remembers where your pointer was so the custom cursor doesn’t flicker between pages. This stays in your browser tab, is not sent anywhere, and disappears when you close the tab.
Accounts, payments, newsletter, commentsNone. The site doesn’t offer them.
03How I use it

I use what you send me to reply, deal with a permission request or bug report, consider a collaboration, and correct project information. I handle an enquiry because you chose to send it and asked for a response. I don’t use your information for unrelated purposes, sell it or use it for advertising.

04Who else handles it

Your email passes through my email provider, Google (Gmail). The site is hosted by GitHub, and fonts come from Google. I don’t share your information with anyone for marketing. I may disclose information if the law requires it.

05How long I keep it

I keep messages only as long as I need them to deal with the enquiry or keep a useful record, and normally delete them within two years of the last message.

06Security

The site is static: it has no database, accounts or forms, so it doesn’t store visitor data itself. Messages you send sit in my email account. No online service is perfectly secure.

07Your rights

You can ask what personal information I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it. Email 29aryash@gmail.com and I will respond within 30 days. India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 is being brought into force in stages, with the main duties due from May 2027. I will follow the rules that apply to me as they take effect.

08Transfers outside India

GitHub and Google may process data in other countries, including the United States.

09Children

The site is a general portfolio and is not aimed at children. I don’t knowingly collect children’s personal information. If you think a child has sent me information, tell me and I’ll delete it.

10Changes

I’ll update this policy if the site or the law changes, and revise the effective date when I do.
