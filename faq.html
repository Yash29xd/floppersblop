FAQ

01
What is FloppersBlop?

An independent creative technology project: Minecraft modpacks and texture packs, software experiments, and notes on how they’re made.

Who runs it?

Yash Arya. It’s one person. FloppersBlop isn’t a registered company and has no staff, investors or clients.

Why the name?

It began as a joke: a dumpyard for a flopper’s ideas, including those that fail, get rebuilt or end up useful.

What have you made?

OptiBoostCraft, a performance-focused Minecraft modpack on Modrinth; RTX Textures, an archived compilation resource pack; and the Growing Channel on YouTube. See the Projects page.

What do you build with?

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python and C++ across my experiments. Each project page lists what that project actually uses.

Is OptiBoostCraft official?

No. It’s an unofficial fan project and isn’t approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft. The mods in it belong to their authors.

Can I download RTX Textures?

No. It is archived, no longer in production, and not offered from this site.

Is OptiBoostCraft still maintained?

Yes. It is still maintained, and the latest release is on its Modrinth page.

Did you make the textures in RTX Textures?

No. It is a compilation of other creators’ texture packs, which I combined and resized. The textures belong to their creators, who are credited on the project page.

Why are the download counts dated?

Because they change, and because a download isn’t the same as an active player or a performance gain.

Can I collaborate with you?

Possibly. Email 29aryash@gmail.com with your idea, what you’d bring and how you’d like to work. I can’t take on everything.

Do you take commissions?

No. Everything here is free and I’m not taking paid work. If that changes, the Services page will say so first.

Can I copy, edit or redistribute your projects?

Check the licence on the project’s page. If there isn’t one, ask first. Third-party mods, textures and assets have their own terms.

Can I use Minecraft-related projects commercially?

Don’t assume you can. Check each project’s licence and the Minecraft Usage Guidelines, and ask me if unsure.

How do I report a bug?

Email 29aryash@gmail.com with the project name, your game or software version, what you did and what happened. Don’t send passwords or payment details.

Does the site use cookies or collect data?

No cookies and no analytics. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the site briefly remembers your pointer position inside your own browser tab. If you email me, I keep your message so I can reply. See the Privacy Policy.

Where can I follow updates?

On YouTube at the Growing Channel, on Modrinth for OptiBoostCraft, in the Discord server (https://discord.gg/QXB922G5W5), and on the profiles listed on the Contact page.
