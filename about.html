About

A little order for a lot of ideas.

What this is

FloppersBlop is a one-person creative technology project. I build software experiments, Minecraft modpacks and texture packs, and I write about how they come together. There is no team behind it, no investor and no company. It is a workshop with a website attached.

The name

FloppersBlop began as a joke about how most of my ideas turn out. I think of it as a dumpyard for a flopper’s ideas: attempts that fail, get rebuilt or end up useful. The blob in the logo is that flopped stuff, dumped in a cardboard box. The box holds the tried-and-failed attempts I keep so I can look back and reflect on where I am now. A lot of the struggle behind the work never shows.

Why I started it

My work was scattered across a Modrinth page, a Modpack Index listing and a YouTube channel. None of them could tell the whole story, or explain why a project exists, what went wrong and what I’d do differently. This site is that missing layer.

How I work

I try to write about what a project actually does, not what sounds impressive. A prototype is allowed to be a prototype, and I say so. Other people’s work is credited and kept separate from mine, which matters a lot in modpacks, where nearly every part comes from another author. Limits such as supported versions, dependencies and known bugs go on the page next to the features. If I say a pack runs better, I want to say on what hardware, in which version and how I checked.

Who’s behind it

I’m Yash Arya, a student based in New Delhi, India. I build with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python and C++, and most of what I know is self-taught. My interests run to PC performance, Minecraft optimisation, Linux and virtualisation, hardware compatibility and cybersecurity. I’m still learning most of it.

Outside this project I started the Growing Channel on YouTube. In 2025 I spent four months as a software intern in industrial automation at Ruian Zhenjing Machinery Private Limited, working on PLC and embedded control software, HMI set-up and documentation. In 2023 I finished 4th in the Delhi Robotics League as a team member and 4th in the TechnoXian World Robotics Championship drone challenge as team leader, and in 2024 I was a core volunteer with Karwaan Mission, handling logistics and photography. None of that makes FloppersBlop a company; it is a place to build things and be honest about how they turned out.
