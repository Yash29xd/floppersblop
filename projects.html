OptiBoostCraft

TypeMinecraft modpack (performance-focused)
StatusReleased on Modrinth and still maintained. Last updated about five months before 26 October 2026.
MinecraftJava Edition 26.1.2, 1.21.1, 1.20.6 and 1.20.4, as listed on Modrinth on 26 October 2026
Mod loaderFabric · client and server
LicenceAll Rights Reserved (pack), as listed on Modrinth. Included mods keep their authors’ licences.
DownloadsAbout 6,400 on Modrinth (shown as 6.4K), as of 26 October 2026
Pagehttps://modrinth.com/modpack/optiboostcraft
Creator profilehttps://modrinth.com/user/OptiBoostCraft

What it is. OptiBoostCraft is a curated set of community-made optimisation mods, configured to work together so the game runs better. A modpack is a bundle of mods that the player installs in one go instead of hunting for each one.

What I do on it. I choose which mods go in, check that they work together, test different configurations, investigate problems people report, publish updated releases and take user feedback into account.

What’s in it. Performance-focused mods from various creators, chosen to improve frame rate, reduce stutter and lag, and keep the visuals intact. Those are the pack’s goals, not measured results. Exact mod list below under Credits.

Downloads. Downloads show how many times the pack was fetched. They don’t tell you how many people still play with it, and they aren’t a measure of performance.

Credits. The mods inside OptiBoostCraft were made by their respective authors and stay under their own licences. The full list is below. Each mod’s author and licence are on its own Modrinth page.

RTX Textures

TypeMinecraft resource pack (Java Edition)
StatusArchived · no longer in production · not offered from this site
First released6 June 2021, for Java Edition 1.7.10 to 1.17.1 as originally described

What it was. RTX Textures was a compilation resource pack. I didn’t create the textures: they come from many other creators’ texture packs. My part was choosing and combining them, and downscaling or upscaling some of the files so the pack looked good and didn’t lag.

What I did. Collected and combined texture packs, and resized some of their files.

Status. The pack is archived. It is not available from this site, and I don’t update or support it.

Credits. Every texture belongs to its original creator. Source packs included NAPP, UMSOEA, etc. If you made one of them and want it credited or removed, email 29aryash@gmail.com.
